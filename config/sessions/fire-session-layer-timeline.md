### Elements transition note

The **ELEMENTS** in this production are not continuous. They are used exclusively as transition layers:

- **10:00-12:00:** Gradual fade-out from the Introduction into Deep Relaxation.
- **19:00-20:00:** Gradual fade-in to prepare the Return.

ELEMENTS therefore behave inversely to the rest of the relaxation section. They fade out at the beginning to hand over to the deep ambient textures, then fade back in at the end to bridge into the Return section.
