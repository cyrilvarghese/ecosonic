# Water Session Layer Timeline

> Values in this file come from the production-structure spec (TRACK INFO). Times are
> approximate as written in the spec ("around minute 3", "between 6 and 7"); `~` marks these.
> Fade-ins and fade-outs average ~1 minute.

## Section 1 - Introduction (0:00-10:00)

| Layer | Starts | Full Level | Starts Leaving | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ELEMENTS | 0:00 | ~1:00 (Fade in) | ~9:00 | End of section |
| NOISE | 0:00 | Immediate | Continuous | End of section |
| ISO | ~1:00 | ~2:00 (Fade in) | ~7:00 | ~9:00 (Fade out) |
| PLANETS | ~2:00 | ~3:00 (Fade in) | ~7:00 | ~9:00 (Fade out) |
| PAD | ~3:00 | ~4:00 (Fade in) | ~9:00 | ~9:00 |
| BASS | ~4:00 | Immediate | ~9:00 | ~8:30-9:00 |
| ARP | ~4:30-5:00 | ~5:30-6:00 (Fade in) | ~9:00 | ~8:30-9:00 |
| MELODY | ~6:00-7:00 | Immediate | ~9:00 | ~8:30-9:00 |

### Notes
- **Sequential/causal entry:** ISO → PLANETS → PAD → BASS → ARP → MELODY, each triggered once the previous reaches full level (BASS enters immediately once PAD is full; no fade-in).
- A **second Element** enters ~5:00 and coexists with the main Element for the rest of the section.
- All musical layers stay active until ~8:30-9:00, then fade out.
- From ~9:00 the musical layers fade out while **ISO and PLANETS begin a fresh ~1-min fade-in** to bridge into Deep Relaxation.
- **NOISE is continuous** and carries uninterrupted into Section 2.

## Section 2 - Deep Relaxation (10:00-20:00)

| Layer | Starts | Full Level | Starts Leaving | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SUB ELEMENTS | 10:00 | Immediate | Continuous | 20:00 |
| NOISE | 10:00 | Immediate | ~17:00 | ~18:00 (Fade out) |
| ISO | 10:00 | Rising to max | ~17:00 | ~18:00 (Fade out) |
| PLANETS | 10:00 | Rising to max | ~17:00 | ~18:00 (Fade out) |

### Notes
- The primary Element is gradually **replaced by SUB ELEMENTS**; ISO and PLANETS keep rising to their maximum.
- **No PAD, BASS, ARP or MELODY** in this section — environmental textures and psychoacoustic layers only.
- **Final 2 minutes (18:00-20:00): only SUB ELEMENTS remain.**

## Section 3 - Return (20:00-30:00)

Mirrors the Introduction: SUB ELEMENTS transition back into the main ELEMENTS, and layers are
reintroduced in the same order. **The spec gives only the order and "mirrors Section 1" — the
times below are derived by mirroring Section 1 (+20:00) and should be confirmed.**

| Layer | Starts | Full Level | Starts Leaving | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ELEMENTS | 20:00 | ~21:00 (Fade in) | ~29:00 | End of session |
| NOISE | 20:00 | Immediate | ~29:00 | End of session |
| ISO | ~21:00 | ~22:00 (Fade in) | ~27:00 | ~29:00 (Fade out) |
| PLANETS | ~22:00 | ~23:00 (Fade in) | ~27:00 | ~29:00 (Fade out) |
| PAD | ~23:00 | ~24:00 (Fade in) | ~29:00 | ~29:00 |
| BASS | ~24:00 | Immediate | ~29:00 | ~28:30-29:00 |
| ARP | ~24:30-25:00 | ~25:30-26:00 (Fade in) | ~29:00 | ~28:30-29:00 |
| MELODY | ~26:00-27:00 | Immediate | ~29:00 | ~28:30-29:00 |

### Notes
- Reintroduction order: **Elements → Noise → ISO → PLANETS → PAD → BASS → ARP → MELODY.**
- The **Elements selected in the Return may vary** depending on the software's selection — Section 3 has a deliberate variable/random dimension.
- The session concludes with a gradual reduction of all layers.
