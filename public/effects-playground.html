A sound stops.
The room keeps going.

That sentence is the whole idea. Everything below is a way to hear it — using the exact reverb and delay code that ships in src/audio/effects.ts, so what you hear here is what your melody tracks are doing.

04

The five words that matter

Reverb
A room. Thousands of reflections smeared into a wash you can't pick apart. Clap in a bathroom — that ring is reverb. Length is how long the ring lasts.
Delay
An echo. Whole, distinct repeats you can count. Shout at a cliff. Time is the gap between repeats; feedback is how many you get before they fade.
Send
How much of a track gets fed into the shared room, 0–100%. There's one room for the whole mix and each track chooses how wet it is. Your melody sits at 75%; everything else at 0%.
Dry / Wet
Dry is the untouched sound. Wet is what comes back out of the room. You always hear both mixed together — the blue and orange traces above.
Post-fade
The send is tapped after the track's volume fader. So when a phrase ends, it stops feeding the room, but the room keeps ringing what it already got. Tap it before the fader instead and the tail would get chopped off with the note — which was the original bug.
